## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The severe thunderstorm outlook of the NOAA Storm Prediction Center at each house of Gladys located in the
  contiguous United States: one device per house with the SPC category (0 to 5) of today, tomorrow and the day
  after, the tornado, hail and wind probabilities of today and tomorrow, the any-severe probability of the day
  after, the conditional intensity group of each hazard and a summary sentence.
- Scene triggers: the risk reaches a category, the risk is upgraded, the tornado probability reaches a level.
- A dashboard widget with the three days, the probabilities and a link to the SPC outlooks.
- Texts, feature names and documentation in English, French, German and Spanish.
